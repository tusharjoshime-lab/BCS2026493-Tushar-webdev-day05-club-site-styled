OUR STORY

Orbit Club is a student-led community built around a simple idea: the universe is more interesting when we explore it together.

✦✧✦

WHY WE EXIST

A space for curious people.

Orbit Club began as an idea shared by students who wanted to make science more social, creative, and accessible. We host informal observing sessions, beginner-friendly talks, and collaborative activities that help members learn from one another.

You don't need to know every constellation or understand advanced physics to belong here. Bring your questions, your enthusiasm, and a willingness to be surprised.

What matters to us

  • Curiosity: questions are always welcome.
  • Inclusion: every student can take part and contribute.
  • Collaboration: we learn better when we share what we know.
